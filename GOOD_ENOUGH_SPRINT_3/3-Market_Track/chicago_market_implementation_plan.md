# CHICAGO MARKET IMPLEMENTATION PLAN
**GOOD ENOUGH™ Market Program | West Loop Location**
*Version 1.0 | July 2025*

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Chicago West Loop Farmers Market represents the flagship launch location for the GOOD ENOUGH™ Market Program. This implementation plan details the complete launch strategy, timeline, and operational requirements to ensure a successful market presence that showcases our premium products while creating an authentic connection to the Chicago community.

## 1. LOCATION PROFILE

### Market Details

| Attribute | Details |
|:---|:---|
| Market Name | West Loop Farmers Market |
| Address | 115 S Sangamon St, Chicago, IL 60607 |
| Market Hours | Saturdays, 8:00 AM - 2:00 PM |
| Season | Year-round (Indoor Oct-Apr) |
| Avg. Weekly Foot Traffic | 3,500-4,200 visitors |
| Demographics | Urban professionals, 25-45, high disposable income |
| Vendor Mix | 40+ vendors (food, craft, specialty goods) |
| Market Management | West Loop Community Organization |
| Booth Fee | $175/week (10' x 10' space) |

### Site-Specific Considerations

1. **Physical Environment**
   - Indoor/outdoor versatility required
   - Limited storage available on-site
   - Power access available (single 15A circuit)
   - High pedestrian visibility corner location secured

2. **Local Regulations**
   - City of Chicago vendor permit required ($125 annual)
   - Sales tax collection and remittance (10.25%)
   - Canopy must be weighted (40 lbs per leg)
   - Signage restrictions (no hanging elements beyond booth perimeter)

3. **Market-Specific Rules**
   - Load-in: 6:00 AM - 7:30 AM
   - Load-out: 2:00 PM - 3:30 PM
   - Vendor parking available 2 blocks away ($15/day)
   - Sustainable packaging strongly encouraged

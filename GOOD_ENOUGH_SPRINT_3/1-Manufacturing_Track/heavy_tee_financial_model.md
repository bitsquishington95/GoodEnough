# HEAVY TEE: FINANCIAL MODEL
**GOOD ENOUGH™ Core Product | Version 2.0 | July 2025**

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Heavy Tee represents our flagship product with strong margin potential across multiple distribution channels. This financial model demonstrates that the product can maintain profitability at various production scales while supporting our unique authentication features and premium positioning. At target production volumes of 10,000 units, we project a blended gross margin of 58% across all channels.

## 8. RECOMMENDATIONS & INSIGHTS

1. **Optimal Initial Production**: Begin with 2,500-5,000 unit run to optimize cost efficiency while limiting inventory risk.

2. **Channel Strategy**: Maintain higher DTC ratio in early stages (60%+) to maximize margins and cash flow while building brand.

3. **Price Protection**: The model supports maintaining price integrity even with cost fluctuations up to 10%.

4. **Market Program Scaling**: Each market booth becomes ROI positive at approximately 5 months, supporting aggressive program expansion after initial validation.

5. **Cash Flow Management**: Structure wholesale agreements with deposits to mitigate production financing requirements.

6. **Production Scaling**: Commit to 10,000+ unit production runs once monthly sales exceed 1,200 units consistently for cost optimization.

7. **Regional Pricing**: Consider regional price adjustments to maintain margins in international markets with higher landed costs.

8. **Licensee Structure**: Current 45/55 split provides attractive economics for both licensees and Good Enough, supporting rapid expansion while maintaining quality control.

9. **Volume Thresholds**: Establish production commitments at 20,000+ annual units to negotiate additional 5-8% COGS reduction.

10. **Seasonal Planning**: Schedule production increases 90 days ahead of peak seasons to capture optimal margin during high-volume periods.

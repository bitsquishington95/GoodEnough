## NFC INTEGRATION

### Integration Location
- Position: Inside left chest interior pocket
- Distance from pocket top: 2" (± 0.125")
- Distance from pocket edge: 1.5" (± 0.125")

### NFC Pocket Construction
- Dimensions: 40mm × 40mm (± 1mm)
- Material: Self-fabric lining material
- Closure: Open top with bartack reinforcement
- Attachment: 4-sided lockstitch to interior pocket panel
- Visibility: Hidden construction with printed indicator

### NFC Chip Specifications
- Type: NTAG 424 DNA
- Size: 15mm diameter
- Protection: PET encapsulation
- Pre-programming: All chips to be programmed prior to insertion
- Testing: 100% scan verification after insertion

### Integration Process
1. Construct hidden NFC pocket during interior pocket assembly
2. Insert programmed NFC tag during final assembly
3. Verify NFC functionality with test scan
4. Apply NFC indicator label adjacent to pocket
5. Document tag ID association with garment SKU

## QUALITY CONTROL

### Critical Measurement Points
1. Chest width at 1" below armhole
2. Center back length from HPS to bottom hem
3. Sleeve length from shoulder to cuff edge
4. Collar height at center back
5. NFC pocket placement and size

### Testing Requirements
- Seam Strength: 15 lbs minimum
- Colorfastness: Grade 4+ to washing, Grade 3+ to light
- Shrinkage: ≤3% after 3 washes
- Zipper Function: 2000 cycles minimum
- Button Attachment: 15 lbs pull test
- Special: NFC functionality testing required

### Defect Classification
**Major Defects** (Automatic Rejection):
- Incorrect NFC implementation
- Holes, runs, or tears in fabric
- Skipped stitches or broken seams
- Incorrect button placement
- Missing or incorrect labels

**Minor Defects** (Acceptable at ≤3%):
- Slight shade variation within tolerance
- Minor thread ends (if not visible when worn)
- Slight label placement variation (within 1/4")
- Minor pressing marks that will relax with wear

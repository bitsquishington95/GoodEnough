## QUALITY CONTROL

### Critical Measurement Points
1. Chest width at 1" below armhole
2. Length from HPS to bottom hem
3. Collar height at center back
4. Sleeve opening width
5. NFC pocket placement and size

### Testing Requirements
- Shrinkage: ≤5% after 3 washes
- Colorfastness: Grade 4+ to washing, Grade 3+ to light
- Seam Strength: 12 lbs minimum
- Torque: ≤5% after 3 washes
- Special: NFC functionality testing required (see NFC Integration Guide)

### Defect Classification
**Major Defects** (Automatic Rejection):
- Incorrect NFC implementation
- Holes, runs, or tears in fabric
- Color not matching approved standard
- Size outside tolerance range
- Missing or incorrect labels

**Minor Defects** (Acceptable at ≤2%):
- Small, unnoticeable stitching irregularities
- Slight shade variation within tolerance
- Minor label placement variation (within 1/4")

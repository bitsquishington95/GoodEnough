# Licensee First Outreach Strategy
## GOOD ENOUGH™ Market Program

### Overview

This document outlines the strategy for identifying, vetting, and making initial contact with potential Market Program licensees in our three target launch cities (Chicago, Portland, and Los Angeles). The goal is to secure 3-5 introductory calls with high-quality candidates by the end of Sprint 2.

### Target Profiles

We are seeking licensees with the following attributes:

#### Primary Candidate Profile
- Established connection to premium farmers markets in target cities
- Experience in retail/merchandising (especially apparel)
- Authentic connection to local culture and community
- Aesthetically aligned with GOOD ENOUGH™ brand values
- Entrepreneurial mindset with operational discipline
- Sufficient capital for initial investment ($18.5K-$22K)

#### Secondary Candidate Types
1. **Market Organizers**: Individuals who run or manage premium farmers markets
2. **Fashion Micro-Retailers**: Small boutique or pop-up operators with established following
3. **Cultural Connectors**: Individuals deeply embedded in local streetwear/fashion community
4. **Existing Brand Representatives**: Those who have successfully represented premium brands
5. **Design Professionals**: Architects, interior designers, or visual merchandisers looking to expand

### Target Markets & Venue Identification

#### Chicago
- **Primary Markets**: West Loop Farmers Market, Logan Square Farmers Market, 61st Street Farmers Market
- **Secondary Venues**: Randolph Street Market, Pilsen Community Market, Wicker Park Farmers Market
- **Local Connections**: Chicago streetwear community, SAIC alumni, local retail collectives

#### Portland
- **Primary Markets**: Portland State University Farmers Market, Hollywood Farmers Market, Shemanski Park
- **Secondary Venues**: Portland Flea, Pearl District Market, Division/Clinton Farmers Market
- **Local Connections**: Pacific Northwest makers community, outdoor lifestyle brands, local design shops

#### Los Angeles
- **Primary Markets**: Hollywood Farmers Market, Santa Monica Farmers Market, Arts District Markets
- **Secondary Venues**: Melrose Trading Post, ROW DTLA, Echo Park Craft Fair
- **Local Connections**: LA fashion district, westside creative community, entertainment industry adjacencies

### Research Methodology

Each potential candidate will be researched through:

1. **Market Observation**: In-person visits to target markets to identify potential operators
2. **Social Network Analysis**: Instagram, LinkedIn, and local cultural publications
3. **Peer Recommendations**: Asking trusted network contacts for referrals 
4. **Market Management Outreach**: Direct contact with market organizers for recommendations
5. **Local Brand Connections**: Consulting with aligned brands for potential partner introductions

All potential candidates will be documented in our standardized intake form tracking:
- Contact details
- Market affiliations
- Relevant experience
- Community connections
- Alignment indicators
- Capital/investment readiness

### Outreach Approach

Our outreach will follow a carefully sequenced approach:

#### 1. Warm Introduction (Preferred)
- Source connection through mutual contact
- Brief email introduction by connector
- Personal follow-up within 24 hours

#### 2. Direct Approach (Secondary)
- Personalized initial email referencing specific aspects of their work
- Instagram DM as a supplementary touchpoint
- Follow-up call if no response within 3 days

#### 3. Discovery Session (In-Person)
- Brief coffee meeting or market walk-through
- Conversation about market landscape and their experience
- Introduction to GOOD ENOUGH™ concept only after establishing rapport

### Pre-Screening Process

Before advancing to a formal interview, each potential candidate will be evaluated through:

1. **Initial Conversation**: Brief call or meeting to assess general fit and interest
2. **Background Research**: Deeper investigation into their business history and reputation
3. **Market Visit**: Where possible, visit their current operation or market presence
4. **Reference Check**: Informal inquiries with mutual connections
5. **Financial Pre-Qualification**: Ensure investment capability without formal financial disclosure

### Selection Criteria Scoring

Candidates will be scored on a 1-5 scale across these dimensions:

| Criteria | Weight | Description |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Market Connection | 25% | Strength and quality of existing market relationships |
| Operational Capability | 20% | Demonstrated ability to execute retail operations |
| Brand Alignment | 20% | Aesthetic and values alignment with GOOD ENOUGH™ |
| Cultural Authenticity | 15% | Genuine connection to local community/culture |
| Financial Readiness | 10% | Ability to make required investment |
| Growth Potential | 10% | Possibility for expanded territory/markets |

### Phase 1 Targets

By the end of Sprint 2, we aim to:

- Identify and research 20-25 potential candidates across all cities
- Make initial contact with 10-15 highest-potential prospects
- Conduct preliminary conversations with 5-8 candidates
- Schedule 3-5 formal interviews for Sprint 3

### Tracking & Documentation

All outreach and conversations will be tracked in our CRM with standardized documentation including:

- Contact records with all communication history
- Meeting notes from conversations
- Assessment scores against selection criteria
- Next steps and follow-up schedule
- Decision points and rationale

### Timeline

| Week | Activity | Deliverables |
|:---|:---|:---|

| Week 2 (Days 1-3) | Follow-up and preliminary conversations | Conversation notes and assessments |
| Week 2 (Days 3-5) | Formal interview scheduling | Calendar of 3-5 confirmed meetings |

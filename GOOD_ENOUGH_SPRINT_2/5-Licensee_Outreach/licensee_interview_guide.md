# GOOD ENOUGH™ Market Program
## Licensee Candidate Interview Guide

This guide provides a structured approach for the formal interview process with potential Market Program licensees. The interview should feel conversational rather than interrogative, while systematically covering all necessary areas of assessment.

### Interview Preparation

**Before the Interview:**
- Review candidate's profile, application, and previous interaction notes
- Research their current business operations and social presence
- Prepare market-specific questions relevant to their city/region
- Have Market Program deck, sample financials, and territory maps available
- Schedule 60-90 minutes in a neutral location or premium video call

**Materials to Bring:**
- Printed candidate profile (internal document only)
- Scoring rubric (for notes during or immediately after)
- Limited sample product photos (physical samples only for final-stage candidates)
- Non-disclosure agreement (if progressing to detailed discussion)
- Market Program overview deck (abbreviated version)

### Interview Flow

#### 1. Introduction & Setting Context (5-10 minutes)
- Casual introduction and appreciation for their time
- Brief explanation of the interview process and goals
- Clarify that this is a mutual exploration, not just a one-way evaluation
- Set expectations about next steps and timeline

#### 2. Candidate Background Exploration (15-20 minutes)

**Professional Experience:**
- "Tell me about your background and how you came to be involved with [their current market/business]."
- "What aspects of your current work do you find most fulfilling?"
- "How would you describe your approach to customer experience?"

**Market Expertise:**
- "How would you characterize the current state of farmers markets in [city]?"
- "What trends have you observed in how people shop at markets in the last 2-3 years?"
- "Which markets in [city] do you think have the strongest premium customer base, and why?"

**Operational Insight:**
- "Walk me through a typical day when you're operating at a market."
- "What systems or processes have you found most helpful in maintaining quality and consistency?"
- "How do you approach staffing and training for market operations?"

#### 3. Brand Understanding & Alignment (15-20 minutes)

**Aesthetic Sensibility:**
- "How would you describe your personal aesthetic when it comes to retail environments?"
- "What brands do you think are doing interesting work at the intersection of premium and accessible?"
- "What does 'minimal' mean to you in the context of a retail experience?"

**Cultural Connection:**
- "How would you describe the unique character of [their city/region]?"
- "What aspects of local culture do you think are underrepresented in current retail experiences?"
- "How do you stay connected to evolving cultural trends in your community?"

**Values Alignment:**
- "What principles guide your business decisions?"
- "How do you think about balancing quality and accessibility?"
- "What role do you think retail plays in community building?"

#### 4. GOOD ENOUGH™ Market Program Overview (15-20 minutes)

**Introduce the concept using the abbreviated deck:**
- Brand positioning and vision
- Market Program structure and model
- Regional capsule approach
- Licensee expectations and support
- Initial investment and projected returns
- Territory and exclusivity approach

**Gauge reactions and address questions throughout the presentation.**

#### 5. Exploration of Mutual Fit (15-20 minutes)

**Practical Considerations:**
- "Based on what you've heard, what aspects of the Market Program resonate most with you?"
- "What potential challenges do you foresee in implementing this in [specific market]?"
- "How would you approach building local awareness and customer base?"

**Financial Readiness:**
- "What are your thoughts on the investment parameters we've outlined?"
- "What timeline would you envision for implementation if we were to move forward?"
- "How would this integrate with your existing business operations?"

**Vision Alignment:**
- "How would you make this uniquely reflective of [city/region] while maintaining the GOOD ENOUGH™ aesthetic?"
- "What does success look like for you in a partnership like this?"
- "Where do you see opportunity for evolution or growth in this model?"

#### 6. Closing & Next Steps (5-10 minutes)
- Answer any remaining questions
- Explain next steps in the process and timeline
- If appropriate, discuss potential market visit or follow-up conversation
- Thank them for their time and insights

### Post-Interview Evaluation

Immediately after the interview, complete the candidate evaluation form addressing:

1. **Overall Impression** (1-5 scale)
2. **Specific Scoring Categories:**
   - Market expertise and connections
   - Operational capability and experience
   - Brand and aesthetic alignment
   - Cultural authenticity and local knowledge
   - Financial readiness and understanding
   - Growth mindset and potential
3. **Key Strengths & Concerns**
4. **Recommended Next Steps:**
   - Advance to market visit / final interview
   - Hold for comparison with other candidates
   - Not suitable for current consideration
5. **Timeline Considerations:**
   - Candidate's readiness to proceed
   - Alignment with launch schedule
   - Territory prioritization

### Evaluation Rubric

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Market Expertise** | Limited market connections; new to scene | Established in 1-2 markets; good relationships | Deep integration in multiple premium markets; influential position |
| **Operational Capability** | Minimal retail experience; learning stage | Solid operational background; some systems in place | Exceptional operational discipline; proven systems and results |
| **Brand Alignment** | Basic understanding of aesthetic; some misalignment | Good grasp of brand vision; mostly aligned values | Intuitive understanding of brand ethos; perfect aesthetic alignment |
| **Cultural Authenticity** | Surface-level community connection | Active in local culture; good network | Deeply embedded cultural connector; tastemaker status |
| **Financial Readiness** | Uncertain about investment capacity | Comfortable with investment parameters | Fully prepared with capital and financial understanding |
| **Growth Potential** | Focused only on initial location | Open to moderate growth over time | Strategic vision for multi-market expansion |

### Red Flags

Watch for these warning signs during the interview:

- Excessive focus on personal brand over GOOD ENOUGH™ representation
- Resistance to operational standards or quality controls
- Unrealistic revenue expectations or ROI timeline demands
- Disconnect between their aesthetic and GOOD ENOUGH™ brand values
- Over-eagerness to modify or "improve" the core concept
- Lack of specific knowledge about their local market dynamics
- Poor listening skills or dismissive attitude toward guidance

### Green Flags

Look for these positive indicators:

- Thoughtful questions about regional storytelling opportunities
- Specific ideas for local market integration while respecting brand guidelines
- Demonstrated history of long-term business relationships
- Clear articulation of personal connection to GOOD ENOUGH™ values
- Balanced perspective on premium positioning and accessibility
- Evidence of community respect and influence
- Operational precision in their current business

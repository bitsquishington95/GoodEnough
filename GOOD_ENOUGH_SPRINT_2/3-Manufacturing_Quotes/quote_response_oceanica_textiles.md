### Materials & Sustainability

We can source 100% GOTS certified organic cotton at 6.5oz (220 GSM) as specified. Our Los Angeles facility uses 100% renewable energy, and our Guadalajara facility uses 70% renewable energy with a plan to reach 100% by 2026.

Water usage in our dyeing process is 40% below industry average, and we use ZDHC-compliant dyes and chemicals. All fabric waste is recycled into new products or donated to textile recycling programs.

### Quality Control

Our quality control process includes:
- Raw material inspection upon receipt
- In-line quality checks at key production stages
- 100% final inspection before shipping
- Defect rate consistently below 1%
- Lab testing for colorfastness, durability, and shrinkage

### About Midwest Heritage Manufacturing

Founded in 1985, Midwest Heritage Manufacturing represents a continuation of Chicago's storied garment manufacturing tradition. Our 38,000 sq. ft. facility in Chicago's West Humboldt Park neighborhood employs skilled craftspeople, many of whom are second-generation garment workers.

- **Years in Operation:** 40 years
- **Production Team:** 65 skilled employees
- **Monthly Capacity:** 20,000 knit garments
- **Client Base:** Premium American-made brands, workwear labels, and heritage sportswear
- **Certifications:** Made in USA Certified, Fair Labor Association member

## Analysis & Recommendations

### Cost Analysis
- **Lowest Cost Option:** Oceanica Textiles offers the most competitive pricing at all volume tiers
- **Highest Cost Option:** Greenthread Apparel is approximately 15% more expensive than Oceanica
- **Middle Option:** Midwest Heritage offers mid-range pricing with American-made quality

### Strategic Recommendation

Based on GOOD ENOUGH's regional capsule approach, we recommend a **hybrid manufacturing strategy**:

1. **Chicago Capsule:** Partner with Midwest Heritage Manufacturing
   - Authentic Chicago production aligns with local storytelling
   - Local production reduces shipping carbon footprint
   - Premium positioning justifies slightly higher cost

2. **Portland Capsule:** Partner with Greenthread Apparel
   - True Portland production enhances regional authenticity
   - Micro-batch capabilities align with limited edition approach
   - Sustainability story resonates with Portland market

3. **Los Angeles and Other Regions:** Partner with Oceanica Textiles
   - Most cost-effective for larger volume production
   - LA production facility for West Coast authenticity
   - Scalability for future growth

### Next Steps

1. Request physical samples from all three manufacturers for quality comparison
2. Schedule facility tours for Chicago and Portland facilities to assess operations
3. Begin sample development with preferred manufacturer(s)
4. Develop a production schedule aligned with regional capsule launch plans

### Additional Considerations

- **Manufacturing Proof Points:** Include manufacturing location on product story cards to enhance regional authenticity
- **Consistency:** Ensure standardized specifications across manufacturers for consistent quality
- **Risk Mitigation:** Using multiple manufacturers reduces supply chain risk
- **Cost Efficiency:** Consider bulk fabric purchasing across manufacturers to reduce costs

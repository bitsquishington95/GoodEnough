MARKET PROGRAM

GOOD ENOUGH™ is seeking select partners to operate branded micro-booths at premium farmers markets in key cities. This is not a mass franchise opportunity — we're building a limited collective of independent operators who embody our brand values and regional authenticity.

Our Market Program combines the heritage of local markets with authenticated premium streetwear, creating a unique retail model that resonates with today's conscious consumers.

Each partner receives exclusive territory rights, regional product capsules, and a turnkey system for success.

THE PROCESS

1. SELECT

2. EQUIP

Partners receive complete booth designs, product allocation, NFC authentication technology, and comprehensive training.

Launch your GOOD ENOUGH™ micro-booth with regional-specific product capsules that tell authentic local stories.

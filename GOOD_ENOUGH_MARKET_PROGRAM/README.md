# GOOD ENOUGH™ — Market Program Starter Plan

⸻

## Project Purpose

> Build a scalable, franchisable system where GOOD ENOUGH can operate branded micro-booths at farmers markets across North America, offering local-capsule apparel with regional character — authenticated, quality-controlled, and culturally relevant.

## Why Farmers Markets?

- **Mindset Alignment:** People who shop at farmers markets already prefer local, expect to pay premium for quality, appreciate story-driven crafted products, and value authenticity over flash.
- **Cultural Advantage:** GOOD ENOUGH's positioning (brutally simple, high-craft, ironic) feels natural in a farmers market versus forced at a luxury mall.
- **Low Friction for Launch:** Farmers markets have very low booth/rental costs compared to retail leases or traditional pop-up shops.
- **Scalability via Franchise:** Local entrepreneurs can license the concept and run GOOD ENOUGH micro-stalls regionally.

## Potential Models

| Model | Description | Pros | Cons |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Franchise Lite** | Sell pre-packed GOOD ENOUGH starter kits (inventory + booth design) to market vendors. | Fast scale, brand control. | Risk of inconsistent execution. |
| **License Local Drops** | License GOOD ENOUGH brand but allow regional "inspired" capsule collabs (e.g., a 'Sonoran Desert Rust Tee' in Arizona). | Deep local loyalty. Higher margins. | Requires tight IP and QC enforcement. |
| **Direct Brand Booths** | GOOD ENOUGH owns and runs the booths itself (at least in big cities). | Absolute control of brand experience. | Harder to scale fast; ops heavy. |

## First 90 Days: Launch Plan

| Phase | Timing | Actions |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Phase 1: Pilot Setup** | Days 1–30 | Choose 1-2 Chicago markets, design booth, finalize Chicago capsule products, train team |
| **Phase 2: Soft Launch** | Days 31–60 | Pop-up at farmers markets every weekend for 4 weekends; collect feedback, test booth operations, validate NFC scanning |
| **Phase 3: Program Refinement** | Days 61–90 | Finalize Franchise Kit (branding, training, capsule options), start recruiting pilot licensees for Denver, Austin, Portland |

## Important Notes for Scalability

- **Capsule Limit Rule:** No more than two local capsules per year per market (keep drops rare, precious).
- **Mandatory NFC Authentication:** Every product must be authenticated through the same chip system, even socks and totes, to reinforce brand trust.
- **Booth Experience Control:** Booth look must be GOOD ENOUGH certified (even wood finish, fonts, etc.). DIY aesthetics must still pass brand standards.
- **Local Storytelling:** Each local capsule has a small card telling the city story behind the colors/textures (2-3 dry, clipped sentences max).

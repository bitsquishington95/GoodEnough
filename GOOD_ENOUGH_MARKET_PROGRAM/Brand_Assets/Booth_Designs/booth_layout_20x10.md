## Key Layout Elements

| Area | Purpose |
|:---|:---|
| **Left Side Gallery** | Heavy Tees on minimal black rack, spaced for gallery feel |
| **Right Side Gallery** | Premium hoodies and coats, elevated positioning |
| **Front Left Corner** | Entry product display (totes, socks) - lower price points |
| **Front Right Corner** | NFC authentication station with iPad or QR display |
| **Center Back Lounge** | Minimalist seating area (2 stools) on central rug |
| **Back Wall** | Single subtle GOOD ENOUGH banner (no other branding) |

⸻

## Visual & Material Specifications

| Element | Details |
|:---|:---|
| Canopy | Matte fabric (Bone color #E7E0D4), no logos on top |
| Flooring | Natural fiber outdoor rug covering entire 20x10 space (bone or olive tone) |
| Racks | Matte black steel, <2" diameter piping, clean minimal lines |
| Tables | Natural unfinished wood tops with black steel legs (brutalist aesthetic) |
| Lounge Seating | 2 minimal wood stools with optional canvas seats |
| Banner | Heavy canvas in Bone color with black GOOD ENOUGH logo (~6'x3') |
| Lighting | 2-4 battery-powered gallery spotlights (neutral white) |

⸻

## Customer Flow Design

1. **Entry** - Full 20-foot front open for approach
2. **Discovery** - Front tables draw customers in with accessible items
3. **Exploration** - Racks guide deeper to premium items (tees → hoodies)
4. **Engagement** - Lounge area creates dwell time and social proof
5. **Authentication** - NFC station allows tech interaction without sales pressure

⸻

## Booth Scale & Placement

| Item | Size | Placement |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Racks | 5'–6' wide | Left and right side walls |
| Tables | 2' deep x 4' wide | Front corners |
| Main Rug | 10' x 18' | Covers booth floor with 1' perimeter |
| Lounge Area | 4' deep | Center back with two stools + mini rug section |
| Banner | 6' x 3' | Center back wall |

⸻

## Key Advantages of 20x10 Over 10x10

- **True Gallery Experience** - Space between products signals intentional luxury
- **Lounge Element** - Creates customer dwell time (increases purchase likelihood)
- **Balanced Visual Weight** - Room for adequate product display without crowding
- **Clear Customer Journey** - Natural flow from entry → browse → engage
- **Product Hierarchy Display** - Space to properly showcase entry vs. premium items

## Strategic Booth Design Goals

- **Space as Luxury** - Intentional negative space signals premium quality
- **Material Tactility** - Natural materials invite customer touch
- **Visual Restraint** - Minimal branding creates intrigue and exclusivity
- **Dwell Time** - Lounge area increases average time spent in booth (and purchase likelihood)
- **Gallery Walkthrough** - Customers flow naturally from affordable items → hero pieces → brand experience

⸻

> **GOOD ENOUGH projects "rare, real, premium"**  
> through **space**, **materials**, **tactility**, and **authenticity** —  
> NOT through flashy signs, "market tricks," or loud selling.

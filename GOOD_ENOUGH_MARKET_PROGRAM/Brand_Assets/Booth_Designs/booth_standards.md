# GOOD ENOUGH™ Market Booth Standards

⸻

## Core Principles

1. **Brutally minimal**
2. **Functionally premium**
3. **Locally authentic**
4. **Intentionally understated**

Every booth must convey these principles without explanation.

## Required Elements

### Structure
- 10'x10' canopy tent (Bone #E7E0D4 or Asphalt #1A1B1D only)
- Raw wood or matte black display table (minimum 4' length)
- No tablecloths—raw materials only
- Minimal hanging rack (black pipe or natural wood)
- No visible branding on structure elements

### Signage
- One 24"x36" GOOD ENOUGH sign (monospace type, left aligned)
- Small 6"x8" location card (e.g., "WEST LOOP")
- NFC authentication station (minimal black stand)
- No additional signage, posters, or promotional materials

### Product Display
- Maximum 3 styles visible at once
- Garments folded in stacks of no more than 5 pieces
- No mannequins or traditional retail fixtures
- Skull embroidery must be visible on at least one displayed item

### Technology
- iPad or tablet for NFC authentication demonstrations
- Square reader or equivalent for transactions
- No visible cables or technology infrastructure
- No music or speakers

## Forbidden Elements
- No plastic fixtures or containers
- No bright colors outside approved palette
- No discount/sale signage
- No traditional retail POS systems
- No branded shopping bags (kraft paper only)

## Approved Materials Palette
- Weathered wood (natural finish)
- Black metal pipe
- Kraft paper (unbleached)
- Canvas (natural)
- Matte black metal
- Concrete

## Photography Guidelines
- Document your booth from the same angle each market day
- Submit photos weekly via the GOOD ENOUGH Market Portal
- Customer photos only with explicit permission
- No "lifestyle" photoshoots without HQ approval

⸻

> Not trying too hard.  
> Not flashy.  
> Just well-crafted, intentional, and quietly confident.

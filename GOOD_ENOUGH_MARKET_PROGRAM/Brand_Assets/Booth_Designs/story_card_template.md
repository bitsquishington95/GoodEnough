# GOOD ENOUGH™ — Story Card Template (Per Product)

⸻

## Front (visible side)

```plaintext
[Product Name]
[Colorway Name]
Cut + Sewn: [City Name]
Fabric: [Fabric Type]
Weight: [oz/yard²]

Authenticated Garment
[NFC symbol or subtle skull icon]
```

### Example:

```plaintext
HEAVY TEE
Lake Effect Grey
Cut + Sewn: Chicago, IL
Fabric: 14oz U.S. Cotton
Weight: 14 oz/yard²

Authenticated Garment
[💀 small icon here]
```

_(Simple, brutal, pure.)_

⸻

## Back (optional, hidden side, small fine print if needed)

```plaintext
GOOD ENOUGH™

This piece was cut, sewn, and dyed locally.
No shortcuts.
No perfect copies.

Each batch varies slightly.
That's the point.

Scan the NFC tag inside for authentication + origin.
```

⸻

## Physical Design Specifications

| Element | Details |
|:---|:---|
| Size | ~4"x6" card or thick cardstock hangtag |
| Font | IBM Plex Mono (8–10 pt) |
| Paper | Heavy stock, muted color (Bone #E7E0D4 or slight Olive tint) |
| Attachment | Natural jute twine or micro binder clips (no plastic fasteners) |
| Placement | Near neck label or on sleeve cuff (visible but not dangling in the way) |

⸻

## Optional Variants

For extra local emphasis without marketing language:
- **City Seal Mark:**  
  e.g., a faint watermark: "GOOD ENOUGH · CHICAGO LOCAL CAPSULE 2025" at the bottom corner.
- **QR/NFC Flow:**  
  A minimalist QR or NFC tap logo pointing to:  
  - Product authentication page  
  - Capsule backstory (2-3 dry facts, not marketing copy)

## Strategic Value

- **Immediately justifies price** without "selling"
- **Reinforces craft value** in one quick glance
- **Creates discovery space** for customers (scan NFC if curious)
- **Premium feel** — no loud hangtags like fast fashion

⸻

> These Story Cards make each garment feel like it came from a **museum gift shop** —  
> not a random T-shirt tent.
>
> Perfectly GOOD ENOUGH.

# GOOD ENOUGH™ NFC Authentication Process

⸻

## Overview

Every GOOD ENOUGH product contains an embedded NFC chip that:
1. Verifies authenticity
2. Provides product history
3. Creates a digital ownership record
4. Unlocks exclusive content

This is not just anti-counterfeit technology—it's our blockchain-backed ownership system.

## Technical Specifications

- NFC Type: NTAG 216 (888 bytes)
- Embedded Location: Left hem, bottom corner (1cm from edge)
- Casing: Textile-safe, washable enclosure
- Mobile Read Compatible: Yes (iPhone and Android)
- Read Distance: 2-4cm

## Customer Authentication Flow

1. **Tap**
   - Customer taps product with smartphone
   - NFC chip activates and connects

2. **Verify**
   - GOOD ENOUGH authentication screen appears
   - Product details display (style, region, drop date)
   - Green checkmark confirms authenticity

3. **Register**
   - Optional ownership registration
   - Confirms first owner and logs to blockchain
   - Creates permanent digital record

4. **Experience**
   - Unlocks product story
   - Reveals local capsule imagery
   - Shows making-of content

## Licensee Setup Process

1. **Scan Inventory**
   - Scan all received products on arrival
   - System logs inventory receipt at your location
   - Confirms products are ready for sale

2. **Demo Authentication**
   - Use booth iPad for customer demonstrations
   - Show authentication process during sales interactions
   - Highlight the digital ownership experience

3. **Transfer Ownership**
   - At purchase, transfer ownership to customer
   - Log sale in GOOD ENOUGH Market Portal
   - NFC system notifies new owner of successful purchase

## Digital Benefits

- **Proof of Authenticity**: Verifies genuine GOOD ENOUGH product
- **Regional Identity**: Confirms market-exclusive capsule
- **Content Access**: Unlocks making-of videos and local stories
- **Community Connection**: Optional entry into GOOD ENOUGH collector database
- **POAP Collection**: First step toward loyalty rewards (future program)

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|:---|:---|
| NFC not reading | Ensure phone's NFC is enabled; scan closer to tag location |
| Authentication failed | Contact HQ immediately; product may be compromised |
| Customer has no NFC phone | Use booth iPad to demonstrate and register |
| Scanner app crashes | Reboot device; ensure latest app version |

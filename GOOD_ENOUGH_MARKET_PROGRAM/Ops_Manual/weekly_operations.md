# GOOD ENOUGH™ Market Program - Weekly Operations Manual

## INTRODUCTION

This operations manual outlines the standard procedures for each GOOD ENOUGH™ market franchisee to ensure consistency across all locations while delivering an exceptional customer experience. Following these guidelines is essential to maintain brand integrity and optimize sales performance.

## WEEKLY SCHEDULE OVERVIEW

| Day | Primary Activities | Time Requirement |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Monday** | Inventory review, SMS planning | 1-2 hours |
| **Tuesday** | Order submission | 30 minutes |
| **Wednesday** | - | - |
| **Thursday** | Shipment receipt, inventory prep | 2-3 hours |
| **Friday** | SMS blast, final prep, pre-market setup | 2-3 hours |
| **Saturday** | Market day operations | 8-10 hours |
| **Sunday** | Reporting, inventory reconciliation | 1-2 hours |

## PRE-MARKET PREPARATION

### Monday: Planning (1-2 hours)

1. **Inventory Assessment** (30 min)
   - Count remaining inventory from previous market
   - Document sell-through rates by category and SKU
   - Identify top performers and slow movers
   - Update inventory spreadsheet

2. **SMS Content Planning** (30 min)
   - Draft weekly drop announcement
   - Select product highlights (include 1-2 hero products)
   - Choose imagery from approved asset library
   - Plan any special promotions or early access opportunities

3. **Performance Review** (15-30 min)
   - Review previous week's sales data
   - Note customer feedback and interactions
   - Document any operational challenges

### Tuesday: Order Submission (30 min)

1. **Inventory Order** (30 min)
   - Log into GOOD ENOUGH™ Portal (franchise.goodenough.co)
   - Navigate to "Weekly Order" section
   - Review recommended order based on sales history
   - Make adjustments based on:
     - Local events
     - Weather forecast
     - Previous week's sales
     - Seasonal considerations
   - Submit final order by 12:00pm local time
   - Save order confirmation number

### Thursday: Receiving & Prep (2-3 hours)

1. **Shipment Receipt** (1 hour)
   - Verify package count matches manifest
   - Inspect packaging for damage
   - Document any discrepancies with photos
   - Sign for delivery

2. **Inventory Processing** (1-2 hours)
   - Unpack all items
   - Check each item against packing list
   - Inspect products for quality issues
   - Organize by product category
   - Enter new inventory into POS system
   - Store in designated containers
   - Report any quality issues or discrepancies

### Friday: Final Preparations (2-3 hours)

1. **SMS Marketing** (30 min)
   - Finalize SMS copy in platform
   - Upload approved images
   - Schedule blast for 6:00pm local time
   - Prepare early access passes (if applicable)

2. **Equipment Check** (30 min)
   - Test POS system and card reader
   - Verify NFC scanner functionality
   - Check booth structural components
   - Ensure all signage is clean and undamaged
   - Charge all electronic devices

3. **Pre-Market Setup** (1-2 hours, if permitted by market)
   - Transport equipment to market location
   - Stage booth components in secure location
   - Verify market space assignment

## MARKET DAY OPERATIONS (SATURDAY)

### Morning Setup (6:00am - 8:45am)

1. **Arrival & Booth Construction** (6:00am - 7:30am)
   - Arrive at market no later than 6:00am
   - Unload all equipment and product
   - Assemble booth structure following booth guide
   - Install all fixtures in designated positions
   - Place signage according to visual standards
   - Set up POS system and NFC station

2. **Product Merchandising** (7:30am - 8:30am)
   - Unpack and steam/prepare all garments
   - Display products according to visual merchandising guide
     - Maximum 3 units per style visible
     - Maintain negative space principles
     - Follow category arrangement guidelines
   - Ensure all products have NFC tags properly placed
   - Prepare early access area (if applicable)

3. **Final Checks** (8:30am - 8:45am)
   - Verify all systems are online
   - Brief any Brand Ambassadors on featured products
   - Review key talking points for new releases
   - Ensure booth perimeter is clean and organized
   - Position early access line marker (if applicable)

### Sales Operations (8:45am - 3:00pm)

1. **Early Access Period** (8:45am - 9:00am)
   - Welcome SMS subscribers with early access
   - Scan early access passes
   - Provide personalized shopping experience
   - Process transactions

2. **General Sales Period** (9:00am - 3:00pm)
   - Maintain consistent customer flow
   - Limit booth to 3-4 customers at once
   - Restock display products as items sell
   - Collect customer information for SMS list
   - Process transactions efficiently
   - Assist with NFC registration
   - Maintain booth organization

3. **Customer Engagement Guidelines**
   - Greet each customer with subtle acknowledgment
   - Provide product information only when requested
   - Focus on quality and craftsmanship narratives
   - Never discuss pricing until point of purchase
   - Emphasize limited availability when appropriate
   - Guide customers through NFC registration process
   - Collect email/phone with customer permission only

4. **Inventory Management During Sales**
   - Maintain "one out, one in" display approach
   - Track sell-through rates in real-time
   - Note customer product requests/inquiries
   - Document product feedback

### Breakdown & Closing (3:00pm - 4:30pm)

1. **Sales Closing** (3:00pm - 3:15pm)
   - Process final transactions
   - Thank remaining customers
   - Close POS system

2. **Inventory Management** (3:15pm - 3:45pm)
   - Count remaining inventory
   - Pack remaining product carefully
   - Document any damaged/display items

3. **Booth Breakdown** (3:45pm - 4:30pm)
   - Disassemble booth following guidelines
   - Clean all surfaces
   - Pack all components in designated containers
   - Load vehicle in reverse order of setup
   - Final site check for any remaining items

## POST-MARKET PROCEDURES (SUNDAY)

### Reporting & Analysis (1-2 hours)

1. **Sales Reporting** (30 min)
   - Log into GOOD ENOUGH™ Portal
   - Submit final sales report including:
     - Total revenue
     - Units sold by category and SKU
     - Sell-through percentages
     - New SMS subscribers added
     - NFC registration rate
     - Customer demographics (if collected)

2. **Inventory Reconciliation** (30 min)
   - Verify physical count against POS data
   - Document any discrepancies
   - Update inventory spreadsheet
   - Prepare preliminary next order based on stock levels

3. **Performance Analysis** (15-30 min)
   - Review key metrics against targets
   - Document customer feedback and insights
   - Note operational challenges or opportunities
   - Complete weekly performance survey

## SPECIAL SITUATIONS

### Weather Contingencies

In case of inclement weather:
1. Check market policy for cancellations
2. If market is canceled:
   - Notify HQ immediately
   - Send SMS to subscribers about cancellation
   - Consider backup location options (if approved)
   - Update social media accounts
   - Prepare rain check incentives for next market

### Inventory Shortages

If demand exceeds supply:
1. Track all customer requests for out-of-stock items
2. Offer to notify customers when item is restocked
3. Never promise specific delivery dates
4. Consider offering similar alternatives if available
5. Report high-demand items to HQ immediately

### Technology Issues

In case of POS or NFC system failure:
1. Switch to offline mode on POS system
2. Use manual credit card imprinter (in emergency kit)
3. Document all transactions in backup spreadsheet
4. Collect customer information for delayed NFC registration
5. Contact technical support through franchise portal

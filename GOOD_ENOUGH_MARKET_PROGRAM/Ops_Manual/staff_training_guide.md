# GOOD ENOUGH™ — Market Booth Staff Training Guide

⸻

## Core Brand Principles

1. **Understated Luxury**  
   - We don't try hard to impress.
   - We let quality speak through materials, not hype.
   - We are minimal, calm, and confident.

2. **Local Craft First**  
   - Chicago-made is our core story.
   - Regional capsules celebrate local identity.
   - We never apologize for higher prices — we explain craft.

3. **Authentic Interaction**  
   - Staff are guides, not salespeople.
   - We inform rather than persuade.
   - We create space for customers to discover at their own pace.

⸻

## Booth Presence: How to Be

| Do | Don't |
|:---|:---|
| Stand casually, hands free | Hover or stare at customers |
| Allow space for browsing | Follow customers around the booth |
| Speak in short, direct sentences | Use marketing buzzwords or hype |
| Be comfortable with silence | Fill quiet with nervous chatter |
| Know your product details cold | Talk about "luxury" or "premium" labels |

⸻

## Customer Interaction Scripts

**Opening Greeting:**  
_"Hey. Feel free to look around."_

**If Customer Asks About Brand:**  
_"We're GOOD ENOUGH. Everything's cut and sewn in Chicago. This collection is specific to [market location]."_

**When Discussing Materials:**  
_"14-ounce cotton. Garment dyed locally. Each piece comes out slightly different."_

**If Asked About Price:**  
_"The tees are $48. Everything's locally made, authenticated with an NFC chip in the hem."_

**NFC Authentication Demo:**  
_"You can tap your phone here to see where it was made and register ownership if you want."_

**When Customer is Deciding:**  
[Say nothing. Give space.]

⸻

## Product Knowledge Essentials

Every staff member must know:

1. **Exact materials** for every product in the booth
2. **Production location** (specific neighborhood if possible)
3. **Story behind regional capsule** colorways
4. **NFC authentication process** (be able to demo flawlessly)
5. **Care instructions** for each fabric type

⸻

## Managing the Booth

**Opening Procedures:**
1. Set up racks with precise spacing (minimum 3" between hangers)
2. Arrange entry product display (totes, socks) with one example opened/displayed
3. Test NFC station functionality
4. Ensure story cards are properly attached to all garments
5. Do a final booth aesthetic check (minimal, clean, intentional)

**During Market Hours:**
1. Maintain product spacing as items are browsed
2. Keep entry product display stocked but never crowded
3. Process sales quickly and efficiently
4. Bag purchases in kraft paper with minimal branding
5. Restock from inventory bins discreetly

**Closing Procedures:**
1. Document sales in GOOD ENOUGH Market Portal
2. Photograph booth from standard angle for quality control
3. Pack inventory in specified order in transport crates
4. Ensure all equipment is properly stowed, especially electronics

## Language Guidelines

| Say | Instead Of |
|:---|:---|
| "Cut and sewn in Chicago" | "Handcrafted luxury garment" |
| "14-ounce cotton" | "Premium quality material" |
| "Limited to this market" | "Exclusive drop" |
| "Individually authenticated" | "Anti-counterfeit technology" |
| "Lake Effect Grey" (specific color name) | "Cool grey color" |
| "Garment-dyed downtown" | "Special dyeing process" |

⸻

## Handling Common Situations

**If a customer asks about the price being high:**  
_"Everything's made locally rather than overseas. The materials are heavier than standard shirts, and each piece is authenticated individually."_

**If a customer asks when you'll have more stock:**  
_"Each capsule is limited. Once they sell out, we move to the next seasonal drop."_

**If a customer asks about other locations:**  
_"We have specific market capsules in select cities. Each one has colorways unique to that location."_

**If someone wants wholesale information:**  
_"We're focused on direct sales right now. I can take your information for our team if you're interested in future opportunities."_

## Key Metrics to Track

1. **Visitor-to-Sale Conversion** (be aware of your ratio)
2. **NFC Scans to Sales** (how many people who scan actually buy)
3. **Entry Product to Hero Product Upsells** (tote/socks → tee/hoodie)
4. **Customer Feedback** (note common questions/comments)
5. **Regional Capsule Performance** (which colorways resonate most)

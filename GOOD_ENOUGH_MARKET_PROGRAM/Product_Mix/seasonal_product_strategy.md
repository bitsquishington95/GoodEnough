# GOOD ENOUGH™ Seasonal Product Strategy

## OVERVIEW

This document outlines the seasonal product mix strategy for GOOD ENOUGH™ Market Program locations. The strategy is designed to maintain brand consistency while allowing for seasonal relevance and location-specific customization.

## PRODUCT MIX PRINCIPLES

### Core vs. Seasonal Balance

| Category | Percentage | Replenishment | Description |
|----------|------------|---------------|-------------|
| Core Products | 60% | Weekly | Staple items available year-round with consistent colorways |
| Seasonal Products | 30% | Quarterly | Items that reflect seasonal needs and trends |
| Local Specials | 10% | Monthly | Location-specific products with regional customization |

### Gross Margin Requirements

All products must maintain minimum gross margins:
- Core Products: 75-80%
- Seasonal Products: 80-85%
- Local Specials: 85-90%

## LOCAL CUSTOMIZATION GUIDELINES

### Regional Color Palette

Each region receives an exclusive custom colorway based on local landscape:

| Region | Primary Color | Secondary Color | Inspiration |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|------------|
| Pacific Northwest | Forest Green | Slate Gray | Temperate rainforests |
| Southwest | Terracotta | Sand | Desert landscapes |
| Midwest | Wheat | Sky Blue | Prairie expanses |
| Northeast | Charcoal | Navy | Urban/coastal environment |
| Southeast | Clay | Moss Green | Subtropical environment |

### Local Embroidery Elements

Each market location receives custom embroidery options:

1. **City Coordinates**: Latitude/longitude coordinates in minimal typography
2. **Regional Flora**: Single-line illustrations of local plant species
3. **Market Name**: Discreet market location identifier

### Collaborative Products

Guidelines for local vendor collaborations:

1. Maximum of one collaboration per quarter
2. Vendor must have minimum 2-year history at selected market
3. Product must utilize both brands' core competencies
4. Limited run of 25-50 units maximum
5. Exclusive to the specific market location
6. Documentation of story/process for booth narrative

## INVENTORY MANAGEMENT

### Stocking Strategy

| Product Type | Initial Stock | Replenishment Trigger | Production Lead Time |
|--------------|---------------|------------------------|----------------------|
| Core Products | 75 units | 25% remaining | 2 weeks |
| Seasonal Products | 50 units | No replenishment | 4 weeks |
| Local Specials | 25 units | No replenishment | 3 weeks |

### End-of-Season Protocol

For seasonal products reaching end of availability:
1. No discounting permitted
2. Last 25% of inventory reserved for loyalty program members
3. Remaining pieces archived for future limited re-release
4. No product ever sold through non-market channels

## PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT TIMELINE

| Phase | Time Before Launch | Activities |
|-------|-------------------|--------------------|
| Concept Development | 6 months | Material sourcing, colorway selection, prototype development |
| Sample Production | 4 months | First article samples, fit testing, durability testing |
| Production | 2 months | Manufacturing, quality control, distribution prep |
| Market Delivery | 1 week | Allocation to markets, booth setup, staff training |

## MERCHANDISING GUIDELINES

### Display Standards

- Maximum of 3 items per linear foot of display space
- Core products at eye level, seasonal at touch level
- Color blocking over product categorization
- Single spotlight per 4 square feet of display
- Minimum 30% negative space in visual displays

### Storytelling Elements

Each seasonal collection includes:
1. Material origin cards
2. Process photography
3. Designer/craftsperson profiles
4. Local relevance narrative

## MEASUREMENT & EVALUATION

### KPIs

| Metric | Target | Measurement Frequency |
|--------|--------|------------------------|
| Sell-through Rate | >90% | Weekly |
| Time to 50% Sell-through | <4 hours | Weekly |
| Repeat Purchase Rate | >35% | Monthly |
| Cross-category Purchase | >45% | Monthly |
| Season-to-Season Retention | >60% | Quarterly |

### Feedback Protocol

1. Staff observations collected weekly
2. Customer feedback digitally through NFC platform
3. Quarterly review of sales velocity by product/color
4. Cross-regional performance comparison monthly

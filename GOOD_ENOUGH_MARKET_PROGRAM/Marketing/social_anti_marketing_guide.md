# GOOD ENOUGH™ ANTI-MARKETING SOCIAL STRATEGY

## CORE PHILOSOPHY

GOOD ENOUGH™ employs an "anti-marketing" approach on social platforms—prioritizing organic discovery, community cultivation, and authentic connection over traditional promotional tactics. This document outlines strategies for maintaining our stealth luxury positioning through deliberately understated digital presence.

## PLATFORM STRATEGY

### Primary Channels

| Platform | Purpose | Content Style | Posting Cadence |
|----------|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| Instagram | Visual documentation | Raw, unedited process imagery | 1-2 weekly, no stories |
| Discord | Community building | Conversational, technical, informative | Daily engagement, no scheduled posts |

### Secondary/Experimental Channels

| Platform | Purpose | Approach | Notes |
|----------|---------|----------|-------|
| Physical Zine | Tangible archive | Quarterly production documentation | Available only at market booths |
| WhatsApp Groups | Local micro-communities | Market-specific connections | Invite-only via booth visits |
| Film Photography | Analog documentation | Developed and shared quarterly | Physical prints at markets only |

## CONTENT PRINCIPLES

### The Un-feed Approach

1. **Process Over Product**: 80% production documentation, 20% finished goods
2. **Technical Detail**: Material specifications, construction methods, maker profiles
3. **Zero Lifestyle**: No styled shoots, influencers, or aspirational imagery
4. **Text-Heavy**: Detailed captions with material origins, technical specifications
5. **Unscheduled**: Random posting patterns that follow production cycles

### Visual Language

| Avoid | Instead Use | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| Product-only shots | Hands making products | Closeup of hand-stitching a collar |
| Styled environments | Raw workshop settings | Cutting table with pattern pieces |
| Model photography | Staff wearing products naturally | Team lunch in sample garments |
| Color correction | Raw, unfiltered imagery | Natural light workshop documentation |
| Brand-forward composition | Material-forward composition | Extreme closeup of fabric weave |

## ENGAGEMENT FRAMEWORK

### Community Guidelines

* **No Reactive Posting**: Never respond to trends or current events
* **Selective Engagement**: Reply only to technical questions and genuine inquiry
* **Information Over Promotion**: Share knowledge freely, never "tease" products
* **Credit Ubiquitous**: Always tag collaborators, suppliers, and makers
* **Zero Aspirations**: Never imply that products will improve lifestyle or status

### The Invisible Algorithm

1. **Non-promotional Tagging**: Technical terms only (#selvedgedenim not #luxuryfashion)
2. **Engagement Disincentives**: No CTAs, questions, or engagement bait
3. **Link Minimization**: Rarely direct to website or purchasing opportunities
4. **Growth Indifference**: No engagement with growth-hacking practices
5. **Randomized Content**: No content themes, series, or predictable patterns

## CUSTOMER AMPLIFICATION

### The Owner Storyteller Model

* **Community Contributions**: Platform for customer craftsmanship and creativity
* **Wear Documentation**: Highlighting how products age through customer submissions
* **Technical Discussions**: Customer-led conversations about product performance
* **Knowledge Exchange**: Platforms for customers to share care and maintenance tips
* **Local Connections**: Facilitating market-specific customer meetups

### Amplification Criteria

| Content Type | Amplification Worthy If | Approach |
|--------------|-------------------------|----------|
| Product Age | Shows 1+ years of honest wear | Unedited repost with technical notes |
| Creative Use | Demonstrates unexpected utility | Featured in "product evolution" documentation |
| Technical Question | Reveals common knowledge gap | Expanded into detailed educational post |
| Local Context | Shows product in specific regional setting | Added to market-specific archive |

## MEASUREMENT & SUCCESS METRICS

### Anti-Growth Indicators

| Metric | Target | Rationale |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Save Rate | >40% of impressions | Indicates reference value over casual consumption |
| Comments:Likes Ratio | >1:10 | Shows meaningful engagement over passive approval |
| Average Comment Length | >15 words | Demonstrates thoughtful interaction |
| Direct Messages | >5% conversion from views | Indicates desire for deeper connection |
| Shares to Private Channels | >Shares to Public | Shows authentic peer recommendation |

### Red Flags

* Sudden follower spikes (indicates potential trend or viral content)
* Engagement from non-target audiences (suggests content drift)
* Increase in "Where can I buy?" comments (indicates failure to educate on process)
* Style-focused comments over technical appreciation (suggests wrong audience attraction)
* Algorithm-favored content (indicates potential alignment with conventional marketing)

## CONTENT CATEGORIES

### Documentation Series

* **Material Genesis**: Following raw materials from source to production
* **Maker Profiles**: Technical backgrounds and specialized skills of team
* **Construction Chronicles**: Step-by-step documentation of product creation
* **Testing Protocols**: Durability and performance testing demonstrations
* **Aging Archives**: Systematic documentation of how products evolve with use

### Knowledge Transfer

* **Material Science**: Detailed explanations of fiber properties and performance
* **Construction Techniques**: Tutorials on methods used in our products
* **Maintenance Manuals**: Proper care procedures for maximum longevity
* **Repair Rituals**: Step-by-step guides for extending product life

## LOCAL MARKET INTEGRATION

### Market-Specific Content

* **Booth Construction**: Documentation of each market's unique booth setup
* **Local Maker Features**: Profiles of craftspeople from each market region
* **Regional Materials**: Highlighting locally-sourced inputs when applicable
* **Community Connections**: Documenting interactions within each market
* **Weather Adaptations**: How products perform in different regional conditions

### Cross-Market Pollination

1. **Materials Exchange**: Documenting the flow of materials between markets
2. **Technique Sharing**: Cross-training between regional production teams
3. **Customer Connections**: Facilitating interaction between markets
4. **Regional Interpretations**: How different markets adapt core products
5. **Seasonal Transitions**: How products move between markets with seasons

## COLLABORATION FRAMEWORK

### Partner Selection Criteria

* Technical excellence in complementary field
* Shared values around transparency and quality
* No existing social media strategy or conventional marketing
* Preference for being discovered rather than promoted
* Commitment to documentation over presentation

### Collaboration Amplification Guidelines

1. **Process-Forward**: Focus on shared techniques and material knowledge
2. **Equal Technical Detail**: Both parties documented with same specificity
3. **No Promotional Language**: Zero "excited to announce" content
4. **Extended Documentation**: Longer-form content over announcement posts
5. **Archive-Focused**: Designed for reference value rather than immediate impact

## CRISIS MANAGEMENT

### Potential Scenarios

| Scenario | Response Approach | Channel |
|----------|-------------------|---------|
| Quality Concerns | Detailed technical explanation of issue and resolution process | Discord primary, Instagram secondary |
| Supply Chain Disruption | Complete transparency about challenges and alternatives | Direct email to customers, Discord backup |
| Copying/Counterfeiting | No public acknowledgment, private education on verification | Direct customer communication only |
| Unwanted Media Attention | No comment, no engagement, continue regular documentation | Continue standard posting, no acknowledgment |
| Viral Attention | Temporary content pause until attention subsides | Maintain Discord engagement only |

### The Disappearing Act

In case of unwanted mainstream attention or significant platform algorithm changes that compromise our approach:

1. **Content Archive**: Move documentation to owned platforms
2. **Community Migration**: Transfer connections to more controlled channels
3. **Temporary Invisibility**: Pause public-facing content during attention spike
4. **Return Strategy**: Gradual reemergence with more technical, less accessible content
5. **Platform Abandonment**: Willingness to leave platforms that force conventional marketing

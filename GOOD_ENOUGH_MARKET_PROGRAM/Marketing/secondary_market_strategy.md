# GOOD ENOUGH™ SECONDARY MARKET STRATEGY

## CORE PRINCIPLES

GOOD ENOUGH™ implements a controlled secondary market strategy to maintain product integrity, preserve brand value, and reinforce our commitment to product longevity. This document outlines our approach to secondary sales and the systems that support it.

## AUTHENTICATION FRAMEWORK

### NFC Verification System

| Component | Implementation | Purpose |
|-----------|----------------|---------|
| Hardware | NXP NTAG 424 DNA chip embedded in product | Tamper-evident authentication |
| Data Structure | Encrypted product history with ownership chain | Verified provenance tracking |
| Verification App | iOS/Android native application | Consumer-side authentication |
| Backend | Blockchain-based ownership ledger | Immutable transaction record |
| Transfer Protocol | Dual-device NFC handshake | Secure ownership transfer |

### Authentication Levels

1. **Basic Verification**: Product authenticity check (available to all)
2. **Ownership Verification**: Current owner status (available to owner)
3. **History Access**: Complete ownership chain (available to current owner)
4. **Service Eligibility**: Repair and renewal qualification (available to current owner)
5. **Resale Clearance**: Authorization for secondary market listing (available to current owner)

2. **Counterfeiting**: Undermines authentication system

# GOOD ENOUGH™ MARKET PROGRAM PRODUCT MIX

## CORE PHILOSOPHY

Our product mix strategy for the Market Program is built on these foundational principles:

| Principle | Implementation | Rationale |
|-----------|----------------|-----------|
| **Deliberate Scarcity** | Limited units per market per week | Creates desire and urgency |
| **Seasonal Resilience** | Core items adaptable across seasons | Minimizes dead stock risk |
| **Local Relevance** | Market-specific finishing and details | Builds community connection |
| **Quality Focused** | Fewer, better items versus variety | Supports premium positioning |
| **Visual Cohesion** | Limited, coordinated color palette | Creates recognizable aesthetic |

## LOCAL CAPSULE FRAMEWORK

### Capsule Definition

Each market location receives a dedicated capsule that includes:

| Component | Quantity | Customization Approach |
|-----------|----------|------------------------|
| **Market-Specific Tee** | 1 design, 20 units | Location name embroidery, local color story |
| **Seasonal Headwear** | 1 style, 15 units | Location detail, regional color influence |
| **Signature Accessory** | 1 item, 15 units | Local artist collaboration, regional materials |
| **Core Items** | 3-5 styles, 10 units each | Standard items with market day stamp |
| **Limited Edition** | 1 specialty item, 5 units | Numbered edition with local significance |

### Customization Methods

| Technique | Application | Equipment | Training |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|----------|
| **Embroidery** | City/neighborhood names, coordinates | Portable embroidery machine | 4-hour technical training |
| **Stamping** | Market day validation, dates | Custom rubber stamps, ink | 30-minute demonstration |
| **Custom Tags** | Local story elements, artist bios | Tag printer, templates | 1-hour system training |
| **Hand Finishing** | Unique details, hand-sewn elements | Basic sewing kit, guidelines | 2-hour technique instruction |
| **Local Material Integration** | Regionally sourced components | Material library, assembly guides | 3-hour fabrication training |

### Rotation Strategy

| Timeframe | Action | Purpose |

| **Quarterly** | New seasonal emphasis | Adapts to climate and needs |

### Dead Stock Prevention

| Risk Factor | Mitigation Strategy | Implementation |
|-------------|---------------------|----------------|
| **Seasonal Mismatch** | Transitional weight core products | 80% of core items suitable for 3 seasons |
| **Size Imbalance** | Data-driven size curves by market | Monthly analysis and adjustment |
| **Color Misses** | Limited palette with proven performance | 5 core colors, 2-3 seasonal additions maximum |
| **Style Failures** | Small initial drops, scale successes | Test with 10-15 units before wider release |
| **Over-production** | Made-to-order system for specialty items | Pre-orders with deposit for limited editions |

# GOOD ENOUGH™ PRODUCT DROP MODEL

## CORE CONCEPT

The GOOD ENOUGH™ Product Drop Model creates a ritualistic, anticipatory shopping experience that rewards consistent engagement with our brand at farmers markets and enhances the value of each product through controlled availability and local relevance.

## DROP CADENCE

### Primary Drop Structure

| Timeframe | Frequency | Scale | Focus |
|-----------|-----------|-------|-------|
| Weekly | Every Saturday | 15-30 units per SKU per market | Core essentials with market-specific details |
| Seasonal | Every 90 days | 50-75 units per collection per market | Complete capsule with location-inspired elements |
| Annual | Once yearly | 100-150 units per special item per market | Signature piece with elevated construction |

### Rhythm Principles

1. **Consistency**: Same day, same time at each market
2. **Transparency**: Published schedule, never "surprise" drops
3. **Predictability**: Core items replenished weekly with minimal variation
4. **Progression**: Building narrative across sequential drops
5. **Ceremony**: Consistent unpacking and display ritual at market opening

## PRODUCT ALLOCATION

### Unit Volume Guidelines

| Product Category | Weekly Units | Seasonal Units | Annual Units |
|------------------|--------------|----------------|--------------|
| Core T-Shirts | 25-30 | 50-60 | N/A |
| Specialty Tops | 15-20 | 30-40 | 75-100 |
| Bottoms | 15-20 | 30-40 | 75-100 |
| Accessories | 20-25 | 40-50 | 100-125 |
| Statement Pieces | 10-15 | 20-30 | 50-75 |

### Allocation Rules

1. **Equal Distribution**: Same baseline allocation to all active markets
2. **Historical Adjustment**: ±10% based on previous sell-through rates
3. **Seasonal Relevance**: ±15% based on regional seasonality
4. **Launch Weighting**: +20% for first 90 days of new market
5. **Special Event Bonus**: +25% for markets with coordinated events

## LOCAL CUSTOMIZATION

### Regionalization Approach

| Element | Implementation | Examples |
|---------|----------------|----------|
| Colorways | Region-specific palette derived from local landscape | Desert tones for Southwest, urban grays for Chicago |
| Embroidery | City name in standardized script placement | "AUSTIN" on left sleeve, "DENVER" on right back shoulder |
| Hang Tags | Location-specific information and imagery | Local landmarks, neighborhood maps |
| Materials | Climate-appropriate variations of core designs | Lighter weights for Southern markets, heavier for Northern |
| Packaging | Market-specific carry bags | Local topographical maps as abstract patterns |

### Customization Constraints

1. **Brand Integrity**: All customizations must adhere to core design language
2. **Production Efficiency**: Modifications limited to late-stage production processes
3. **Material Consistency**: Base fabrics remain constant across all markets
4. **Design Cohesion**: Regional variations visually relate to each other
5. **Subtle Differentiation**: Variations noticeable primarily to owners and enthusiasts

## INVENTORY MANAGEMENT

### Stock Position Principles

1. **Controlled Scarcity**: 85-95% sell-through target per drop
2. **Core Continuity**: Essential items available consistently though never surplus
3. **Specialty Progression**: One-time drops that evolve rather than repeat
4. **Archive Management**: Select items held for future special releases
5. **No Backstock**: Products move forward to next market rather than accumulating

### Sell-Through Response Matrix

| Sell-Through Rate | Action | Timeframe |
|-------------------|--------|-----------|
| >95% | Increase allocation by 10-15% | Next drop |
| 85-95% | Maintain current allocation | Next drop |
| 75-85% | Decrease allocation by 10-15% | Next drop |
| <75% | Reassess product-market fit | Immediate |
| <50% | Remove from drop schedule | Immediate |

## FORBIDDEN PRACTICES

1. **Flash Sales**: No discounting to accelerate inventory movement
2. **Random Drops**: No unscheduled or surprise product releases
3. **Artificial Scarcity**: No deliberate manipulation of availability
4. **Cross-Market Disparity**: No premium products exclusive to certain markets
5. **Preferential Access**: No VIP early access outside established program

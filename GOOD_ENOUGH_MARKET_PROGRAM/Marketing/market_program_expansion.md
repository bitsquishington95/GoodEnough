# GOOD ENOUGH™ MARKET PROGRAM EXPANSION

## DISTRIBUTION MODEL EVOLUTION

### The Saturday Morning Ritual

| Element | Implementation | Customer Experience |
|---------|----------------|---------------------|
| **Weekly Cadence** | Fixed Saturday morning drops, announced 48 hours prior | Creates anticipation and habit formation |
| **Limited Availability** | 15-30 units per location per week | Encourages early arrival and decisive purchasing |
| **Fresh Experience** | New product or color variation each week | Rewards repeat visits and discovery |
| **Community Connection** | Familiar faces and relationship building | Transforms transaction into social ritual |
| **Location Consistency** | Same booth position and setup each week | Builds spatial memory and comfort |

## MARKET SELECTION CRITERIA

### Target Market Profile

| Attribute | Criteria | Measurement |
|-----------|----------|-------------|

### Market Type Categorization

| Market Type | Characteristics | Examples | Approach |

| **Tourist Destination** | Visitor traffic, souvenir potential | Pike Place (Seattle), French Market (NOLA) | Location-specific items, packable products |

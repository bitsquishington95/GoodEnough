# GOOD ENOUGH™ LOCAL MARKET STRATEGY

## MARKET SELECTION FRAMEWORK

### Primary Selection Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Measurement |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Demographic Alignment | 30% | Income brackets, education levels, consumption patterns |
| Market Atmosphere | 25% | Vendor quality, visitor density, overall aesthetics |
| Operational Viability | 20% | Cost structure, schedule compatibility, space availability |
| Growth Potential | 15% | Market trajectory, neighborhood development, tourism trends |
| Competition Profile | 10% | Absence of similar offerings, complementary brands |

### Market Archetypes

| Type | Characteristics | Strategic Approach |
|------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Premium Urban | High visitor count, affluent shoppers, established reputation | Full product range, premium presentation, higher volume |
| Emerging Neighborhood | Growing audience, younger demographic, developing reputation | Core product focus, community integration, growth mindset |
| Destination/Tourism | Varied visitors, experience-seekers, less consistency | Exclusive items, storytelling emphasis, souvenirs approach |
| Specialty/Niche | Dedicated following, theme-specific, passionate attendees | Targeted product selection, deeper storytelling, collaborative focus |
| Seasonal/Event | Time-limited, high energy, celebration atmosphere | Collection launches, limited editions, event-specific offerings |

## MARKET EXIT CRITERIA

Should a market location prove unsustainable, a structured exit process will be implemented to preserve brand equity and customer relationships.

### Exit Triggers

| Trigger | Threshold | Evaluation Period |
|---------|-----------|-------------------|
| Revenue Performance | Below 75% of projections | 3 consecutive months |
| Profit Margins | Below 15% | 6 consecutive months |
| Customer Growth | Negative growth | 2 consecutive quarters |
| Operational Challenges | Unsolvable logistics issues | After 3 mitigation attempts |
| Market Conditions | Deteriorating vendor quality | 6-month decline trend |

### Exit Protocol

1. **Internal Review**: Full analysis of performance and contributing factors
2. **Remediation Attempt**: Structured 90-day improvement plan implementation
3. **Decision Confirmation**: Final evaluation based on remediation results
4. **Customer Communication**: 30-day notice with alternative purchase options
5. **Partner Transitions**: Structured handoff of local relationships
6. **Brand Protection**: Controlled narrative around market transition
7. **Community Appreciation**: Closing event celebrating market participation

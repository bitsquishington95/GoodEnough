## 1. Franchise Fee Structure

A GOOD ENOUGH Market Franchise creates value beyond standard farmers market booths through:

- **Brand Identity** — Scarcity and national credibility with luxe streetwear audience
- **Exclusive Regional Capsules** — Products tied to local identity and stories
- **Supply Chain Access** — Wholesale inventory, NFC authentication, quality materials
- **Online Revenue Share** — Participation in digital sales (unusual for market vendors)

**Pricing Structure:**

| Franchise Type | Price Range |
|:---|:---|
| Standard farmers market booths (crafts, soaps) | $5,000–$10,000 |
| Premium artisan booth brands | $10,000–$20,000 |
| **GOOD ENOUGH Market** | **$15,000–$25,000** |

**Recommended Franchise Fee:**
→ **$18,000** for 3-year license term
→ Early adopter discount: $15,000 for founding licensees (pre-2026)

## 3. Revenue Sharing Structure

Franchise revenue includes both in-person sales and online commission:

| Revenue Stream | Model | Details |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **In-Person Booth Sales** | Wholesale Model | Licensee purchases inventory at wholesale price, keeps 100% of booth sales |
| **Online Regional Capsule Sales** | Commission Model | 10% commission on online sales of their regional capsule |

**Regional Capsule Commission Details:**
- Applies only to products specific to licensee's region (e.g., "Chicago Capsule")
- Does not apply to core GOOD ENOUGH products
- Paid quarterly based on verified online sales
- Tracked through region-specific SKUs in e-commerce system

This hybrid model incentivizes both in-person success and regional brand building.

⸻

## 4. Legal and Operational Structure

| Component | Details |
|:---|:---|
| **License Term** | 3 years, renewable if performance standards are met |
| **Performance Minimums** | Must sell at least $30,000/year or host minimum 12 market days/year |
| **Inventory Requirements** | Required minimum seasonal order (Core + Regional Capsules) |
| **Brand Standards** | Booth must follow GOOD ENOUGH design specifications with quarterly verification |
| **Transfer Rights** | License cannot be sold/transferred without GOOD ENOUGH HQ approval |
| **Revocation Clause** | Early termination possible for market absence or brand standards violations |

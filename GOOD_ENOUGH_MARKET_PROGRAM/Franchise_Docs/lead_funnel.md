## 2. Short Value Proposition (1–2 Sentences)

_"Bring GOOD ENOUGH to your farmers market.  
Offer premium local capsule drops — built for your city, your people, your craft."_

- No bullet lists. No clutter. 
- Use IBM Plex Mono font, small, wide-tracked.

## 6. Backend Organization Tip

When a form is submitted:
- Capture it into a simple Airtable or Notion table:
  - Applicant Name
  - Location
  - Farmers Market Info
  - Status (New / Approved / Declined / Follow-up)
- Bonus: Tag applicants by region to easily plan capsule production geographically.

⸻

## Visual Vibe
- No fake excitement. No "join the movement!" cheesy lines.  
- Clean, dry, almost corporate-feeling — like you're *screening people quietly,* not begging them to join.
- Monochrome base — slight Olive or Rust accent when hovering over form fields/buttons.

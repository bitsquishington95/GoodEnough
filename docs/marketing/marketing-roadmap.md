# GOOD ENOUGH™ — 18-Month Marketing Roadmap

## Marketing Phases

| Phase | Timing | Objective | Tactics | KPI Gate |
|-------|--------|-----------|---------|----------|
| Tease | M-3→M-2 | Seed curiosity | 8 bill-posters (Chicago, NYC, LA) reading "good enough?", QR to cryptic microsite | 5k email sign-ups |
| Reveal | M-1 | Establish brand codes | IG carousel of oversized skull embroidery close-ups; press kits to Hypebeast, Highsnobiety, GQ Style | 15 media pickups |
| Drop 0 | Launch month | Drive scarcity | Pop-up in West Loop (48 hr) · 150 units only · numbered tags | 90% sell-through |
| Sustain | M+1→M+6 | Build community | "Tuesday Is Fine" IG Reels (dry WIP clips) · monthly newsletter ("Probably Worth Reading") · local studio visits | 30% open rate |
| Capsule I | Month 7 | Prove range | Pop-up plus rooftop look-book shot (skull mark hides on sleeve hem) | $75k capsule GM |
| Scale | Month 9-18 | Geographic growth | Wholesale to KITH Chicago + BEAMS Japan · Shopify Plus global | $1.4M DTC gross |

## Measurement Framework

| Metric | Target by month 12 |
|--------|-------------------|
| Unaided awareness (Chicago) | 12% in target demo |
| Instagram followers | 90k (4% ER) |
| First-drop sell-through | 100% < 72 hr |
| Returning customer rate | ≥ 32% |
| Gross margin blended | ≥ 58% |
| PR impressions | 120M |

Monthly analytics deck reviews vs. targets; adjust spend ±15%.

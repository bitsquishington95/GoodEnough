# GOOD ENOUGH™ — Franchise Model Concept

## Overview

The GOOD ENOUGH franchise model enables locally represented clothing styles based on region or city. This model allows for:
- Local entrepreneurs to sell at farmers markets and community events
- Profit sharing from online sales of their specific city/regional products
- Building authentic community connections in each market

## Franchise Structure

### Franchise Partner Benefits
- Exclusive rights to sell GOOD ENOUGH products in defined geographic area
- Profit sharing on online sales originating from their region (tracked via geo-targeting)
- Co-branded marketing materials with regional flair
- Access to exclusive regional capsule collections
- Training and support from the core brand team

### Franchise Partner Responsibilities
- Meet minimum quarterly sales targets
- Attend local markets, events, and pop-ups
- Maintain brand standards and presentation
- Collect and provide customer feedback
- Participate in regional marketing initiatives

## Implementation Timeline

1. **Phase 1 (Months 1-12):** Establish core brand in Chicago with direct brand control
2. **Phase 2 (Months 13-24):** Pilot franchise model in 3-5 select cities
   - NYC
   - Los Angeles
   - Atlanta
   - Portland
   - Austin
3. **Phase 3 (Months 25-36):** Roll out to additional 10-15 markets based on performance data
4. **Phase 4 (Year 4+):** Consider international expansion with similar model

## Franchise Economics

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Initial investment | $15,000-$25,000 (inventory, displays, marketing) |
| Revenue split | 70% franchise / 30% parent on in-person sales |
| Online attribution | 25% commission to franchisee for online sales in territory |
| Marketing contribution | 5% of gross sales for regional/national campaigns |
| Exclusive territory | Population-based (typically 250,000-500,000 people) |

## Technology Integration

- Custom POS system tracking franchise sales
- Geofenced online attribution for proper revenue sharing
- Authentication technology to prevent counterfeit/gray market sales
- Shared CRM database with privacy controls

## Legal Framework

- Franchise disclosure document (FDD) required
- Geographic territory definition with clear boundaries
- IP licensing agreement with quality controls
- Profit-sharing structure with quarterly settlements
- 3-year initial agreement with renewal options

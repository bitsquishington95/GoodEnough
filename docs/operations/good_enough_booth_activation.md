# GOOD ENOUGH™ Booth Activation Plan

## Booth Layout & Design

### Physical Structure
- **Total Space**: 10x20 split model
- **Materials**: 
  - Premium canvas canopy (neutral tones)
  - Natural wood platform (6-8" elevation)
  - Minimal metal framing
  - Soft fabric dividers

### Zone 1: Retail Gallery (10x10)
- **Layout**:
  - Minimalist product racks
  - Single-item displays
  - Natural wood tables
  - Soft lighting
- **Product Display**:
  - Heavy tees (3 colors)
  - Crewnecks (2 colors)
  - Coach jackets (seasonal)
  - Tote bags (stacked)
- **Branding**:
  - "Good Enough." above entrance
  - No price tags
  - No promotional signage

### Zone 2: NFC Passport Lounge (10x10)
- **Layout**:
  - Elevated platform
  - 8 minimalist chairs
  - Standing tables
  - Natural fiber rug
- **Access Control**:
  - NFC pedestal scanner
  - Green/red LED feedback
  - No visible security
- **Amenities**:
  - Local cold brew station
  - Sparkling water cooler
  - Local bakery collab snacks
  - Quiet playlist system

## Activation Protocol

### Setup Procedure
1. **Pre-Market (2 hours before)**
   - Unload and assemble canopy
   - Install platform and dividers
   - Set up NFC system
   - Stock beverage station
   - Test lighting and sound

2. **30 Minutes Before Open**
   - Final product arrangement
   - Beverage temperature check
   - Playlist start
   - Staff briefing

### Staff Roles & Scripts

#### Retail Attendant
- **Dress Code**: GOOD ENOUGH™ tee (no visible branding)
- **Script**:
  - "Touch it."
  - "Still good."
  - "Tap to enter."
- **Rules**:
  - No hard selling
  - No price discussions
  - Minimal interaction

#### Lounge Guardian
- **Dress Code**: Same as retail
- **Script**:
  - "Tap if you know."
  - "Water's cold."
  - "Still good?"
- **Rules**:
  - Verify NFC only
  - No entry without tap
  - Maintain quiet atmosphere

### Customer Flow

#### Entry Points
1. **Retail Side**
   - Open to all
   - Self-guided browsing
   - Minimal staff presence

2. **Lounge Access**
   - NFC verification required
   - One-time tap per visit
   - No re-entry without re-tap

#### Exit Strategy
- No checkout line
- Self-service payment
- Natural flow out

## Daily Operations

### Opening Protocol
- **Time**: Market open - 1 hour
- **Tasks**:
  - Beverage restock
  - Product arrangement
  - System checks
  - Staff briefing

### Peak Hours Management
- **Capacity Control**:
  - Max 8 in lounge
  - Natural rotation
  - No time limits
- **Beverage Service**:
  - Self-serve only
  - No staff interaction
  - Natural restock

### Closing Protocol
- **Time**: Market close + 30 minutes
- **Tasks**:
  - Gentle customer transition
  - System shutdown
  - Inventory check
  - Cleanup

# GOOD ENOUGH™ — Brand Platform

## Purpose
Free people from perfectionism—prove "good enough" can be exceptional.

## Vision
Become the reference point for quietly premium street basics in North America within 5 years.

## Mission
Craft brutally simple apparel, manufactured transparently in the U.S., and tell the story with dry wit.

## Values
- Radical honesty
- Craft before hype
- Local first
- Under-statement

## Positioning Statement
"GOOD ENOUGH is luxury streetwear for people who hate luxury streetwear."

## Audience Archetype
25-40 y/o urban creatives, $90-180k income, values local craft, owns Aimé Leon Dore hoodies but mocks overt flex culture.

## Tone of Voice
- Clipped, monospace copy ("this is fine.")
- Self-deprecating humor ("probably worth it.")
- Zero adjectives about quality—let materials speak.

## Identity System Elements
- **Primary Logo:** Monospace caps GOOD ENOUGH · 8 pt tracking · left-aligned block.
- **Authentication Mark:** ¾ in matte-rayon skull + ¼ in lowercase good enough stacked.
- **Palette:** Asphalt #1A1B1D · Bone #E7E0D4 · Olive #5B604F · Rust Accent #9D5C3C
- **Type:** Primary: IBM Plex Mono · Secondary: Neue Haas Grotesk

**Usage rules:** no main-chest prints; marks sit at left-hem (tees) or cuff (hoodies). Graffiti textures reserved for collateral, not garments.

## 1. Key Cultural Shifts (2–3 Big Trends)

**Format:**  
> _Trend Title (1 sentence summary)_  
> _Short paragraph (2–3 sentences explaining relevance)_  
> _→ Brand Action: [How GOOD ENOUGH should respond or capitalize]_

**Example:**
> **Trend:** _Post-Influencer Trust Collapse_  
> **Summary:** Consumers increasingly distrust highly polished influencers; micro-communities and gritty, imperfect storytelling now outperform staged content.  
> → **Brand Action:** Double down on low-fi "Tuesday Is Fine" WIP Reels; consider piloting a community "skull scout" program for authentic UGC.

⸻

## 2. Consumer Behavior Insights

**Format:**  
> _Insight Title (1 sentence)_  
> _Supporting Data (quote stat or finding)_  
> _→ Brand Action: [Tactical adjustment]_

**Example:**
> **Insight:** _Returning customers now expect "hidden rewards" in DTC streetwear._  
> **Supporting Data:** 64% of Gen Z consumers say they are more loyal to brands that offer hidden perks. (Morning Consult, 2025)  
> → **Brand Action:** Launch hidden capsule unlock via NFC authentication chips for returning customers only.

⸻

## 3. Brand Inspirations & Analogies

**Format:**  
> _Reference Brand or Project (1 line summary)_  
> _Why It's Relevant to GOOD ENOUGH (short paragraph)_  
> _→ Creative Takeaway: [Optional idea spark]_

**Example:**
> **Reference:** _Jacques Marie Mage eyewear's ultra-minimal storytelling with obsessive detail in handmade process videos._  
> **Why Relevant:** GOOD ENOUGH can adopt similar cinematic "making of" storytelling for capsule drops without overt selling.  
> → **Creative Takeaway:** Commission 45-second black-and-white sewing footage teaser for Capsule I.

⸻

## 4. Technology & Commerce Shifts

**Format:**  
> _Tech Trend Title (1 sentence summary)_  
> _Impact on Retail/Fashion_  
> _→ Brand Action: [Strategic note for GOOD ENOUGH]_

**Example:**
> **Trend:** _DTC brands beginning to issue blockchain-backed loyalty points instead of traditional punch cards._  
> **Impact:** Moves loyalty from internal systems to portable, customer-owned records; early adopters build greater trust.  
> → **Brand Action:** Research feasibility of a GOOD ENOUGH loyalty POAP collectible tied to first 500 customers.

⸻

## 5. Risk Warnings or Red Flags

**Format:**  
> _Risk (short title)_  
> _Description (2–3 lines)_  
> _→ Mitigation Plan: [One action to offset risk]_

**Example:**
> **Risk:** _Overextension into too many retail partners by Year 2._  
> **Description:** Diluting brand scarcity risks turning GOOD ENOUGH from a cult brand to "just another streetwear label."  
> → **Mitigation Plan:** Cap retail partners at 5 premium boutiques globally in Year 2; DTC must remain >80% of revenue.

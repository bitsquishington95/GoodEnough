## Manufacturing

Domestic cut-and-sew (GIL Sewing) keeps margin ≈ 55% while protecting from apparel tariffs.

## Product Features

- Ultra-minimal design
- Chicago cut-and-sewn
- Authenticated by micro-embroidered 💀 good enough mark
- Premium materials with focus on weight and texture
- Focus on consistent fit and durability

## Product Growth Plan

1. Begin with core essentials that demonstrate quality and brand ethos
2. Expand to seasonal capsules for storytelling opportunities
3. Introduce accessory items as add-ons and loyalty rewards
4. Consider limited regional variants to test franchise concept

## Quality Control

- Rigorous standards for all manufacturing partners
- 6-week QC buffer for all production runs
- Only announce drop dates when inventory is confirmed in warehouse

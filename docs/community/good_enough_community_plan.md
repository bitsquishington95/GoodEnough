# GOOD ENOUGH™ Community Activation Plan

## Phase 1: Foundation Building (Months 1-3)

### NFC Passport System
- **Entry Point**: First purchase unlocks NFC passport
- **Verification**: Tap at any GOOD ENOUGH™ booth
- **Access**: Health Bar entry, early drops, city circles
- **Tracking**: Wear count, city visits, loyalty status

### City Circle Structure
- **Platform**: SMS-based (minimal, no app download)
- **Size**: Max 200 members per city
- **Content**: 
  - Drop previews (24h before public)
  - Local event invites
  - City-specific stories
  - Secret location hints

### Booth Rituals
- **Daily**: Free water for passport holders
- **Weekly**: Story drops (local history, product origins)
- **Monthly**: Coffee walks with local roasters
- **Seasonal**: City-specific capsule previews

### Loyalty Challenges
- **City Hopper**: Visit 3+ cities = custom piece
- **Wear Warrior**: 10+ verified wears = graphite thread upgrade
- **Story Collector**: Document 5+ booth visits = leather passport card
- **Culture Keeper**: Attend 3+ local events = golden thread chance

## Phase 2: Engagement Framework (Months 4-6)

### Voting System
- **Frequency**: Quarterly
- **Topics**: 
  - Next city thread color
  - Secret tag phrases
  - Local collab partners
  - Health bar additions
- **Rules**: 
  - One vote per passport
  - 24h voting window
  - Results never announced

### Drop Mechanics
- **Graphite Tier**: SMS 1h before public
- **Secret Drops**: Random Sunday locations
- **City Exclusives**: Limited to local circle
- **Golden Threads**: Random distribution in core pieces

### IRL Moments
- **Monthly**: 
  - Farmers market meetups
  - Coffee walks
  - Gallery nights
- **Quarterly**:
  - Chef collab dinners
  - Artist studio visits
  - Maker workshops

### Cultural Programming
- **Partners**:
  - Local chefs
  - Independent artists
  - Boutique makers
  - Wellness studios
- **Format**:
  - Small talks
  - Hands-on workshops
  - Private viewings
  - Collaborative drops

## Phase 3: Scale & Sustain (Months 7-12)

### Ambassador Program
- **Selection**: Top 5% of circle members
- **Roles**:
  - Event hosting
  - Content creation
  - Community moderation
  - Local insights
- **Rewards**:
  - Custom pieces
  - Early access
  - Travel opportunities
  - Golden thread priority

### Content Strategy
- **User-Generated**:
  - Quiet reposts
  - No tagging required
  - Minimal editing
  - Natural moments
- **HQ Content**:
  - Behind-scenes
  - Maker stories
  - City features
  - Drop previews

### Loyalty Tiers
1. **Passport Holder** (Entry)
   - Basic booth access
   - Free water
   - Drop notifications

2. **City Regular** (3+ verifications)
   - Leather passport card
   - Early access
   - Local events

3. **Culture Keeper** (10+ verifications)
   - Golden thread chance
   - Travel opportunities
   - Custom pieces

4. **Ambassador** (Invite-only)
   - All above + hosting rights
   - Travel budget
   - Custom collections

## Community Voice Guidelines

### Tone Rules
- Never say "community"
- Never use exclamation points
- Never over-explain
- Never beg for engagement

### Example Messages
- "Still here. Still good."
- "Found another one. Nice."
- "Tap if you know. (If not, tap anyway.)"
- "Water's cold. You're welcome."

### Content Rules
- No staged photos
- No forced smiles
- No obvious branding
- No trend chasing

## Measurement Framework

### KPIs
- Passport activations
- Circle engagement
- Event attendance
- Secondary market value
- Golden thread sightings

### Success Metrics
- 70% passport retention
- 50% circle participation
- 30% event attendance
- 2x retail resale value
- 5 golden thread sightings/month

- 10 active city circles

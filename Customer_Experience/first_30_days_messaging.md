# GOOD ENOUGH™ — FIRST 30 DAYS MESSAGING BLUEPRINT
⸻

## BRAND VOICE PRINCIPLES

- **Understated**: Never try too hard
- **Minimal**: Economy of words, no marketing fluff
- **Cryptic**: Create intrigue through partial information
- **Dry Humor**: Subtle wit, never obvious jokes
- **Human**: Should sound like texts from a cool acquaintance, not a brand

⸻

## MESSAGING GUIDELINES

- **Timing**: Send between 10am-5pm local time only
- **Frequency**: Maximum twice per week after first 30 days
- **Style**: No emojis, no exclamation points, minimal punctuation
- **Links**: Never include in first 60 days of membership
- **Photos**: Text only for first 90 days, then minimal image use

## 4. Construction Details

### Measurements (Inches)
| Size | XS | S | M | L | XL | XXL |
|------|----|----|----|----|-----|-----|
| **Chest Width** | 19.5 | 20.5 | 21.5 | 22.5 | 24 | 25.5 |
| **Body Length** | 27 | 28 | 29 | 30 | 31 | 32 |
| **Sleeve Length** | 8 | 8.25 | 8.5 | 8.75 | 9 | 9.25 |
| **Shoulder Width** | 17 | 18 | 19 | 20 | 21 | 22 |
| **Neck Opening** | 6.5 | 6.75 | 7 | 7.25 | 7.5 | 7.75 |

### Seams & Stitching
- **Side Seams:** Double-needle chain stitch (3/16" gauge)
- **Shoulder Seams:** Double-needle chain stitch (3/16" gauge) with tape reinforcement
- **Sleeve Hems:** Double-needle coverstitch (1/4" gauge)
- **Bottom Hem:** Double-needle coverstitch (1/4" gauge)
- **Neck Binding:** Clean finish with topstitch (1/8" gauge)
- **SPI (Stitches per Inch):** 10-12

⸻

## 5. Garment Details & Trims

### Labels & Branding
- **Main Label:** 1.5" × 2" printed cotton label at inside back neck with:
  - GOOD ENOUGH logo
  - Size
  - "Made in USA"
  - Care instructions (symbols only)
- **Hem Label:** 1" × 1" printed cotton tag at inside left bottom hem with small skull logo
- **NFC Chip:** Embedded at inside left hem, 3" from side seam

### Special Details
- **Core Version:** Minimal clean finish throughout
- **Regional Versions:**
  - Chicago: Hidden "CHI" embroidery (tonal) on left sleeve
  - Denver: Tonal mountain outline on back right shoulder
  - Austin: Small star detail on left chest
  - Portland: Rain drop detail at back neck

## 7. Quality Control Standards

### Critical Measurements (Tolerance ±0.25")
- Chest width
- Body length
- Sleeve opening

### Critical Appearance
- Color consistency
- Stitch uniformity
- Label placement
- Fabric quality (no slubs, runs, or discoloration)

### Testing Requirements
- Wash test (3 cycles, cold water): Max shrinkage tolerance of 5%
- Color fastness test: Grade 4 minimum (AATCC scale)
- Seam strength test: 15 lbs minimum

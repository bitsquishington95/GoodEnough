# GOOD ENOUGH™ — CITY CIRCLES FRAMEWORK
⸻

## CONCEPT

City Circles are micro-communities built around each GOOD ENOUGH™ city presence. Unlike traditional brand communities, Circles are intentionally small, understated, and focused on cultural connection rather than product hype.

## CIRCLE STRUCTURE

### MEMBERSHIP TIERS
- **Owner** — Has verified at least one GOOD ENOUGH™ product
- **Regular** — Has attended 3+ events or logged 30+ wears
- **Core** — Has 5+ products or attended 10+ events
- **Nomad** — Member of multiple City Circles

### OPERATIONAL MODEL
- No formal "community manager" title or role
- Local staff serve as Circle points of contact
- Members with Core status can initiate gatherings
- HQ sets rhythm but local execution drives authenticity

⸻

## COMMUNICATION CADENCE

| Frequency | Channel | Purpose | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Weekly** | SMS | Location updates, wear reminders | Minimal, factual |
| **Monthly** | Discord | Event announcements, Circle data | Conversational, dry |
| **Seasonal** | Direct Mail | Physical materials for highest tier | Unexplained, artifact-based |
| **Irregular** | "Dark Posts" | Limited-time messages via NFC | Cryptic, rewarding |

⸻

## ENGAGEMENT MECHANICS

### GATHERING TYPES
- **Coffee Walks** — Casual 30-minute morning meetups, no agenda
- **Workshop Sessions** — Skill-building with local makers (textile, craft, food)
- **Archive Viewings** — Limited access to unreleased samples or archived pieces
- **Cultural Excursions** — Gallery visits, architectural tours, local experiences

### CROSS-CIRCLE ENGAGEMENT
- Traveling members receive SMS when entering a new Circle's city
- Guest status automatically granted to any Circle member visiting another city
- Annual "convergence" rotating between cities for highest tier members

## CIRCLE GROWTH MODEL

- Initial Circle size capped at 150 members (Dunbar's number)
- When cap is reached, new sub-Circles form rather than expanding
- Geographic expansion only when natural density exists
- No growth targets or membership drives

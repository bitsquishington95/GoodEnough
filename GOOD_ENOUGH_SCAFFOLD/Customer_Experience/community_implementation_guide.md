# GOOD ENOUGH™ — COMMUNITY IMPLEMENTATION GUIDE
⸻

## APPROACH

The GOOD ENOUGH™ community strategy deliberately inverts traditional community building:

- Product-first, not marketing-first
- Access through ownership, not open enrollment
- Minimal communication, maximum meaning
- Local density over global scale
- Cultural value over commercial transactions

⸻

## IMPLEMENTATION SEQUENCE

| Phase | Timeline | Focus | Success Metrics |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Foundation** | Month 1-2 | NFC system deployment, messaging setup | 40% tap activation |
| **Local Density** | Month 3-5 | City Circle establishment, first events | 15% event attendance |
| **Engagement Loop** | Month 6-8 | Wear tracking, milestone rewards | 3+ monthly taps per user |
| **Cross-Pollination** | Month 9-12 | Multi-city connections, nomad program | 10% multi-city engagement |

⸻

## OPERATIONAL REQUIREMENTS

### TECHNOLOGY
- NFC tag integration in all products (see technical specs)
- SMS automation platform with location capabilities
- Discord server with private channels per city
- Minimal web presence - verification portal only

### STAFFING
- No dedicated "community managers"
- Retail staff serve as Circle facilitators
- Product team responsible for NFC implementation
- Design team creates physical milestone rewards

### PARTNERS
- Local coffee providers for gathering spaces
- Cultural venues for member access
- Selected makers/artisans for workshops

## ANTI-PATTERNS TO AVOID

| Don't | Instead |
|---|---|
| Call it a "community" | Let members name what they're part of |
| Push content marketing | Pull authentic moments from real interactions |
| Scale for scale's sake | Maintain density and quality over growth |
| Reward social posting | Reward physical presence and product wear |
| Create FOMO tactics | Create genuine scarcity through quality |

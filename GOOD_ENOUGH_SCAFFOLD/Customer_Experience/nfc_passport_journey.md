# GOOD ENOUGH™ — NFC PASSPORT USER JOURNEY
⸻

## OVERVIEW

The NFC Passport is the cornerstone of our community strategy - a product-first approach where ownership grants access. No public signups, no traditional "community marketing" - just a quiet, exclusive experience that rewards our most engaged customers.

⸻

## TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

### NFC IMPLEMENTATION
- NTAG 424 chips embedded in all products
- Unique ID tied to product details (city, season, colorway)
- Wear counter increments only once per 24hr period
- Geolocation captured only during tap (for city verification)

### DATABASE STRUCTURE
- UserID linked to phone number (hashed)
- ProductID collection with wear counts
- CityCircle memberships
- Event attendance history
- Milestone achievements

### COMMUNICATION CHANNELS
- SMS primary (no app required)
- Optional Discord for more engaged community
- No email marketing

# Market Program Performance Metrics
**GOOD ENOUGH™ Retail Analytics | September 2025**

## Executive Summary

This report analyzes the performance of GOOD ENOUGH™'s market program approach across 18 months of operations spanning 12 distinct market events in 8 cities. Our proprietary market-based retail model has demonstrated exceptional performance across all key metrics compared to both traditional retail and pure e-commerce channels. The data confirms that our rhythmic, modular approach delivers superior unit economics while building brand momentum through regular customer engagement.

Key findings include:
- 72% higher sales per square foot compared to permanent retail locations
- 2.8x higher customer conversion rate vs. traditional retail settings
- 45% increase in average order value compared to e-commerce
- 94% reduction in fixed overhead costs vs. permanent retail
- 53% higher customer data capture rate with 78% opt-in for ongoing communications

This data validates our market program strategy as both financially superior and strategically advantageous for building the GOOD ENOUGH™ community and brand through consistently memorable retail experiences.

The market program model should remain GOOD ENOUGH™'s primary retail expression, evolving toward a fully integrated physical-digital ecosystem that balances:

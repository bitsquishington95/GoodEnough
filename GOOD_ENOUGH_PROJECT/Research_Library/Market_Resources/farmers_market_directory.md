## Market Selection Criteria

When evaluating potential markets for GOOD ENOUGH™ presence, assess the following criteria:

1. **Demographic Alignment**: 25-45 age range; urban professionals; cultural creatives
2. **Vendor Mix**: Artisanal focus; craft emphasis; minimal mass-produced items
3. **Market Philosophy**: Community-oriented; quality-focused; sustainability values
4. **Location Quality**: Urban accessibility; neighborhood character; visual setting
5. **Operational Cadence**: Regular schedule aligned with target frequency models
6. **Space Availability**: Minimum 10'x10' for standard booth; adjacent to high-traffic areas
7. **Management Structure**: Professional organization with clear permitting process
8. **Weather Contingency**: Indoor options or weather protection for seasonal markets

## Permitting & Regulations

|--------|---------------------|-----------|------------|-------|

| **Austin** | Temporary Vendor Permit; Food Enterprise Permit | 2-4 weeks | $100-350 | Additional requirements for on-site activities |

**Note:** Requirements may vary by specific market. Always contact market management directly for most current information.

## Market Program Calendar Considerations

| Season | Opportunities | Challenges | Strategic Approach |
|--------|---------------|------------|-------------------|
| **Winter (Dec-Feb)** | Less competition; dedicated customer base | Weather; reduced foot traffic in outdoor markets | Focus on indoor markets; pop-up collaborations |
| **Spring (Mar-May)** | Increasing traffic; new product introduction | Variable weather; seasonal transition | Gradual build-up; weather contingency planning |
| **Summer (Jun-Aug)** | Peak attendance; outdoor opportunity | Heat considerations; vacation gaps | Core program execution; expanded hours |
| **Fall (Sep-Nov)** | Steady traffic; holiday preparation | Seasonal competition; weather transition | Cross-market coordination; holiday strategy |

## Technology Integration Notes

- Verify WiFi/cellular connectivity at each market location for NFC verification
- Identify power access points for digital displays and heat-press equipment
- Map GPS coordinates for precise location tagging in city-specific garment data
- Document lighting conditions for consistent booth photography and social content

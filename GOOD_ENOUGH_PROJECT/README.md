# GOOD ENOUGH™ — Project Framework Overview

⸻

## Purpose

This organized system:

- Stores research, product specs, marketing assets, and manufacturing info
- Tracks brand evolution (campaigns, drops, press, influencer seeding)
- Enables fast referencing for decision-making
- Prepares the groundwork for future AI agents to search, suggest, and even predict

## Directory Structure

```
/GOOD_ENOUGH_PROJECT
│
├── /Research_Library
│   ├── Foundational_Studies    — Cultural and psychological research
│   ├── Cultural_Models         — Applied research models, behavioral psychology
│   ├── Brand_Inspiration       — Art, design, and competitor references
│   └── Emerging_Trends         — Future technology and commerce shifts
│
├── /Product_Development
│   ├── Tech_Packs              — Technical specifications for manufacturing
│   ├── Material_Specs          — Fabrics, trims, and material standards
│   ├── Capsule_Plan_Sheets     — Plans for limited product drops
│   └── NFC_Authentication_Designs — Authentication chip specifications
│
├── /Marketing_Campaigns
│   ├── Tease_Phase             — Pre-launch posters, microsites, mystery elements
│   ├── Reveal_Phase            — Brand code establishment, press kits
│   ├── Drop_0_Launch           — Initial West Loop pop-up materials
│   └── Capsule_Campaigns       — Ongoing marketing for new product drops
│
├── /Press_and_PR
│   ├── Press_Kits              — Media materials and brand storytelling
│   ├── Media_Outreach_Logs     — Tracking press contacts and coverage
│   └── Press_Clippings         — Archived press mentions and features
│
├── /Sales_and_Distribution
│   ├── Shopify_Structure       — E-commerce platform setup
│   ├── 3PL_Information         — Fulfillment partner details
│   ├── Wholesale_Accounts      — Tracking retail partnerships
│   └── Global_Expansion_Plans  — International growth strategy
│
├── /Customer_Community
│   ├── Email_CRM_Lists         — Customer database and segments
│   ├── Loyalty_POAP_Plans      — Blockchain authentication and loyalty plans
│   └── Engagement_Analytics    — Metrics on customer interaction
│
└── /Org_Ops
    ├── Team_Structure          — Organizational structure and roles
    ├── Contracts_and_IP        — Legal documents and IP protection
    └── Budget_and_Finance      — Financial tracking and projections
```

## Getting Started

1. Navigate through the directory structure to find relevant content
2. Use the Cursor AI search feature to quickly locate information
3. All documents follow standard markdown format for consistency
4. Key metrics and KPIs are tracked in their respective sections
5. Brand guidelines apply to all documents and assets
